# Baqaya — ASO-Optimised Store Listing (India-first, KSA second)

_Paste-ready rewrite of the Play Console listing. Supersedes the copy in `STORE_LISTING.md`; that file stays as reference/history. Goal: fix the "only 50 installs" problem by (a) putting real keywords where Google weights them most, and (b) leading every surface with one sharp hook instead of "all-in-one money app."_

## What's changing and why

Three ASO levers, in order of impact:

1. **Title carries the highest-volume keyword.** Google weights title keywords far above everything else. The current title ("Baqaya: Budget & Khata") spends 8 characters on an unknown brand and skips the single biggest search term users actually type. A new app has no brand equity to protect yet, so the title should buy discovery, not identity.
2. **One hook, repeated everywhere.** The magic moment is *"your bank SMS becomes an expense automatically, and nothing leaves your phone."* That is the line that wins the first screenshot, the short description, and the first sentence of the long description — not a feature list.
3. **Localised listings + first three screenshots.** A Hindi/Arabic listing is what makes you show up for Hindi/Arabic searches at all; the first 2–3 screenshots are what convert the impression once you do. These two do more than any copy tweak.

Run these as **Play Console "Store listing experiments"** (A/B) where possible — especially the title and first screenshot — rather than assuming. Even at low traffic the title test will read directionally.

## Screenshot plan (the biggest conversion lever after the title)

Order matters most for the first three — most users decide there. Caption each in-image (large text, high contrast), don't rely on the raw screen. Suggested sequence and caption:

1. **Hook — auto SMS.** Show the SMS-consent → expense prefill. Caption: *"Bank SMS → expense, automatically."*
2. **Privacy.** Show the "no account / on-device" state. Caption: *"Your money data never leaves your phone."*
3. **Monthly breakdown.** The dashboard donut/trend. Caption: *"See exactly where your money goes."*
4. **Khata.** Caption: *"Never forget who owes you."*
5. **Split bills.** Caption: *"Split any bill, settle on WhatsApp."*
6. **Zakat / Savings circle.** Caption: *"Zakat calculator + savings circles, offline."*
7. **Languages.** Caption: *"Hindi, Arabic & 12 more."*

Localise the caption text per listing (Hindi captions for the Hindi listing, Arabic for Arabic). This alone typically lifts install rate more than any wording change.

Captions — Hindi: 1) *"बैंक SMS → खर्च, अपने आप"* · 2) *"आपका डेटा सिर्फ आपके फोन में"* · 3) *"पैसा कहां गया, साफ दिखे"* · 4) *"कौन आपका उधार रखता है, याद रहे"* · 5) *"बिल बांटें, WhatsApp पर हिसाब"*.

Captions — Arabic: 1) *"رسالة البنك ← مصروف تلقائيًا"* · 2) *"بياناتك لا تغادر هاتفك"* · 3) *"شاهد أين يذهب مالك"* · 4) *"حاسبة الزكاة، بدون إنترنت"* · 5) *"قسّم الفاتورة وسوِّ عبر واتساب"*.

## Two things to fix in Play Console while you're in there

- **Icon + screenshots must be recaptured** from the current build (new Baqaya icon + decluttered dashboard). A listing whose icon differs from the installed app's icon reads as a bait-and-switch and depresses conversion.
- **The ads-vs-growth tension.** The listing currently sells "free, ad-supported." At 50 users, ad revenue is negligible and a banner on the first session measurably hurts early retention. Consider softening or removing the ad emphasis in copy, and revisiting whether ads should even be on before you have a few thousand engaged users — this ties directly into the monetisation sequencing (grow first, then Pro).
