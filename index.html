Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

Effective Date: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Expense Tracker ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is managed.

Important: Expense Tracker does not operate any backend servers, does not require user accounts, and does not upload your financial data to the internet. Your information remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

The Short Version

Expense Tracker has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising software. All expenses, categories, budgets, and settings are stored locally on your device.

The developer does not collect, access, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal or financial information.

What the App Stores and Where

The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Expense records
  • Categories and budgets
  • User display name
  • Language preferences
  • Currency preferences
  • Application settings

All information is stored using a local Room/SQLite database on your device.

If you uninstall the application, all locally stored data is removed. You may also permanently delete your data at any time using:

  • Settings → Reset All Data

PDF Export and Sharing

Expense Tracker allows users to export reports as PDF files.

Any sharing of exported reports is initiated solely by the user through Android's native sharing functionality. Once shared, the privacy practices of the selected third-party application (email, cloud storage, messaging app, etc.) will apply.

The developer has no access to exported files and cannot view or recover them.

SMS-Based Expense Detection (Optional)

Expense Tracker may provide an optional feature to detect expenses from SMS messages using Android's SMS User Consent API.

This feature operates under the following privacy protections:

  • The app does not receive blanket access to your SMS inbox.
  • The app cannot read historical SMS messages.
  • Android displays a consent dialog before sharing any message.
  • The app only receives a message after you explicitly approve access.
  • Message processing occurs locally on your device.
  • No SMS content is transmitted to any external server.

Approved messages are used solely to extract transaction information such as merchant name and transaction amount to help create expense entries.

Permissions

The application may use Android system permissions and APIs strictly for providing its core functionality.

Any permission request will be presented by Android and can be accepted or denied by the user at any time.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not upload your expense data.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share user data with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising networks.
  • We do not use analytics tracking services.
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not track users across apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not specifically directed toward children. Since the application does not collect personal information, no personal data is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised effective date.
