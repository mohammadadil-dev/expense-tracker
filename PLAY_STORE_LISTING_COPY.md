```
Baqaya is a simple, private expense tracker that works completely offline. No account, no signup, no data leaving your phone — just open it and start tracking.

Built for India and Saudi Arabia, with everything you actually need in one app.

💸 DAILY EXPENSE TRACKER
Log expenses in seconds, organise them by category, and see exactly where your money goes each month. Set a monthly budget and get alerts before you overspend. Track your income and salary too.

🏠 SHARED BILLS — SPLIT BY DAYS STAYED
The fair way to split apartment electricity and water bills. Add the flats or people who share the bill, mark anyone who travelled, and Baqaya splits the cost by how many days each person was actually there — not a flat equal split. Perfect for shared accommodation and building utilities.

👥 SPLIT BILLS WITH FRIENDS
Create a group for a trip, dinner or shared purchase, add expenses, and see who owes whom. Settle up with one tap and share the breakdown on WhatsApp.

📒 KHATA / UDHAR LEDGER
A digital khata book for shop credit and personal lending. Track what customers owe you and what you owe others, attach bill photos, set credit limits, and send polite payment reminders on WhatsApp.

🔄 COMMITTEE / SAVINGS CIRCLE
Track a committee, kitty or jam'iya: who has paid each round, whose turn it is to collect, and how much the pot is. Send round reminders to members.

🕌 ZAKAT CALCULATOR
Enter your gold and silver in grams with the current price and Baqaya calculates the value and your Zakat automatically, with nisab guidance.

💳 SAUDI & INDIA PAYMENTS
Save your IBAN (the bank name is detected automatically) or your STC Pay number and it's included in every payment reminder you send. Indian users can share a UPI ID and request payments by QR code.

📅 SUBSCRIPTIONS
Keep every subscription in one place — Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, ChatGPT, mobile recharge and more — with monthly, 3-month, 6-month or yearly billing cycles, and see your true monthly cost.

📊 REPORTS & INSIGHTS
Monthly trends, category breakdowns, spending insights, savings goals, and PDF/Excel export for your records.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync and no tracking of your financial data. Optional fingerprint lock keeps the app private, and you can back up and restore your data yourself.

🌍 14 LANGUAGES
English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Filipino and Tagalog.

Whether you call it an expense manager, spending tracker, budget planner, khata book, udhar bahi khata or bill splitter — Baqaya keeps your money organised, offline and private.

Free to download. No account required.
```

## §4 Screenshots — captions to bake into the images

Play shows 2–3 screenshots in search results. Those first two decide most installs, so put a short
headline **inside** the image (large text, top third). Order matters:

| # | Screen | Caption |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Dashboard | Track every rupee — works offline |
| 2 | Shared Bills result | Split bills by days stayed |
| 3 | Khata reminder on WhatsApp | Send payment reminders on WhatsApp |
| 4 | Splits group | Split trips & dinners with friends |
| 5 | Committee | Track your committee & savings circle |
| 6 | Zakat calculator | Calculate Zakat in seconds |
| 7 | Language list | 14 languages · no account · private |

For the **Arabic** listing, re-shoot these with the app in Arabic (RTL) and Arabic captions — a Saudi
user seeing an Arabic UI in the screenshots converts far better than an English one.

Also required/recommended:
- **Feature graphic** 1024×500 — app name + "Expenses · Khata · Split bills · Zakat" + a phone mockup
- **App icon** 512×512 (already have)
- Optional but effective: 30-second **promo video** showing Shared Bills doing the day-based split

## §5 Two things to watch after launch

1. **Play Console → Acquisition → Store listing performance.** Look at *store listing visitors →
   installs* conversion rate. If visitors go up but conversion stays flat, the problem is
   screenshots/description. If visitors themselves stay flat, it's keywords/localisation.
2. **Ratings count.** Ratings are a ranking and conversion factor. You already prompt after the 5th
   expense; with more installs this compounds. Never buy ratings — Play detects it and it can get the
   app suspended.
